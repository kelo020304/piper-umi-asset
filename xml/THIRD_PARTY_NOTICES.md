# Sources and license scope

- Custom gripper geometry: user-supplied `PIPER-UMI-GRIPPER.STEP`, exported by SolidWorks 2022 as AP214 on 2026-09-28. Retained parts: drive/155 mm rail, camera base, sliders, white supports and light apricot-yellow fingers. The former `UMI-PIPER-CAMERA-MOUNT` is removed.
- Arm appearance: 76 material-separated OBJ files from user-supplied `piper_textured_meshes.zip` (base through link5). The archive has no material library; diffuse colors were recovered by matching its material identifiers to the official Piper DAE files: https://github.com/agilexrobotics/piper_ros/tree/noetic/src/piper_description/meshes/dae . Labels are mesh geometry; no external texture images are required. Recovered colors are authored in each model format, so the original missing `material.mtl` is not a runtime dependency.
- Stock link6 output disk, stock flange, six-axis joint definitions, and nominal arm/flange inertias: AgileX `agx_arm_urdf`, commit `f6642ce0d7872c686f29c99e9e10cd23d1d49313`, `piper/meshes/{link6,flange}.stl` and `piper/urdf/{piper_description.urdf,piper_with_gripper_description.xacro}`. Upstream: https://github.com/agilexrobotics/agx_arm_urdf
- `AGILEX_LICENSE.txt` preserves the agx_arm_urdf root MIT license; `PIPER_ROS_LICENSE.txt` preserves the piper_ros root MIT license. These apply to their respective upstream material, not automatically to every user CAD or mesh asset in this package. User-provided assets retain their source rights; no blanket CAD license is asserted here.
- Each standalone folder includes `source_manifest.json` with source hashes and provenance; colors are embedded in the model.
- USD uses OpenUSD Physics and NVIDIA PhysX mimic; MuJoCo uses joint equality for the same ideal parallel motion. Software runtimes are installed separately.
